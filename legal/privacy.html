Privacy Policy

The short version

  • From Google we read only your name and email.
  • We get read-only access to the repositories you pick, only to scan them.
  • Your code is processed to run your scan. It is never sold and never used to train AI models.
  • Access tokens are encrypted, and your reports are private to you.
  • You can disconnect your repositories or ask us to delete your data at any time.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, run scans you request and deliver reports.
  • To keep it secure and prevent abuse, such as fake accounts made for free scans.
  • To support you when you contact us.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Your source code

  • We only access repositories you select, with read-only permission. We never push, commit or change anything.
  • Your code is processed by our AI provider during the scan. Once the scan's usage has been recorded, usually within minutes and at most 2 hours after the scan ends, we delete the scan session, including the code it saw, from our AI provider. Our AI provider may keep API data for a limited time under its own policy, for example to detect abuse. The per-scan access credentials are deleted when the scan ends.
  • We do not keep a copy of your repository after the scan. We keep the report, which may quote short code snippets to explain a finding.
  • Your code and reports are not used to train AI models, by us or by our AI provider.
  • No one on our team reads your code or reports unless you ask us to for support, or the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • GitHub and Azure tokens are stored encrypted in a secrets vault, never in plain database columns, and never in your browser.
  • Database rules ensure each account can only access its own scans and reports.
  • Reports sit in private storage and are downloaded through short-lived links.
  • The extension holds only your sign-in session. It never stores API keys or repository tokens.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Source code: only while the scan runs; the scan session at our AI provider is deleted within 2 hours after the scan ends.
  • Repository tokens: until you disconnect the provider in the extension, or we delete your account at your request.
  • Reports and scan history: until you ask us to delete them or your account, by emailing nocodefinishers@gmail.com.
  • Technical logs: up to 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect GitHub or Azure DevOps at any time, in the extension or in your GitHub or Microsoft account settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or delete your whole account, by emailing nocodefinishers@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act or California law) you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Browser extension data

The extension stores only your sign-in session and which report you last viewed, in Chrome's local extension storage. Its permissions are used only to sign you in, connect your repository, check scan progress, notify you when a scan finishes and download your report. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
